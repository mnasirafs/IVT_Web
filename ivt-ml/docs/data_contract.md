## Grain and keys (verified)
* Sell and buy history: exactly **one line per (CurrentWeek, jno)** in the last 8 weeks (609,991 sell jobs, 613,585 buy jobs). Every job has a single customer and carrier, and a jno never repeats across weeks.
* Current week: 90,718 sell jobs have exactly one buy line; 1 sell job has no buy; **29,219 buy-only jobs** (costs or credits with no sell line, totalling about −$443k) are kept as entities with `MissingSellFlag`.
* `tmp.CurrentTradingData` is sell-driven: 90,728 rows for 90,719 jobs. The 9 duplicated jobs come from MoveIT matching two records. Its sell total equals `Sell_Side` within $148 (the vSellSide connote rewrite); its buy total excludes buy-only jobs.
* `TransactionKey = sha256("k1|Customer|Carrier|JobNumber")` and `SnapshotKey = sha256("k1|TransactionKey|SnapshotWeek|SourceSystem")`. `PrimaryKeyField` is kept only as lineage in `ML.SourceRowMap`, because it is regenerated on refresh.
* Identifier normalisation trims and upper-cases (the IVT collation `Latin1_General_CI_AS` is case-insensitive). Leading zeros (160 connotes and 51 postcodes in the current week) and suffixes are preserved; raw values are kept.

## Consolidation rules
* Each side is aggregated to (week, customer, carrier, job) **before** joining. Charges are summed and line counts kept. Measurements take the maximum across lines and are never summed; `MeasurementConflict` is set when lines disagree.
* Match states: `EXACT`, `MAPPED` (a single buy group with a different code), `AMBIGUOUS` (several groups: buy amounts withheld and no labels), `UNMATCHED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
* MoveIT matches the connote first after IVT's `dbo.fn_strip_connote_suffix` (`MAPPED`), then the full connote (`EXACT`). Conflicting MoveIT records are `AMBIGUOUS` and not used.
* Byte-identical repeated source rows are counted once (`REPEATED_SOURCE_ROW`).
* Totals are reconciled per week: sell lines = snapshot sell, and buy lines = snapshot buy + quarantined buy. Any difference raises a **BLOCKER**. The verified current week reconciled exactly: sell $3,494,730.10 and buy $2,475,039.45.

## Value parsing
* Amounts and measurements are `VARCHAR` in buy/sell tables. They are parsed explicitly, and unparseable values become missing with a data-quality issue (0 found in the last 8 weeks).
* `cons_date` is verified **day-first**: `dd-mm-yyyy` (68%) and `d-mm-yyyy` (32%). Month-first is never guessed; an invalid value sets `InvalidDateFlag`.
* `invoice_date` is a **100% placeholder** (`"/  /"`) and is not used.
* IVT's `Nett_Margin` and `FSL_%` columns are `FORMAT(..., '#,##0.00%')` **strings**. They are never model inputs: margins and ratios are recomputed from numeric amounts. `parse_percent` exists only for reconciliation checks.
* NZPOST buy fuel = `total_nett − freight` (replicated from the fetcher view, so it matches IVT screens).
* Negative amounts form the `CREDIT_REVERSAL` segment (about 2,000 buy lines per 8 weeks, and 13,077 snapshots in the current week). All-zero amounts form `ZERO_VALUE`. Neither is scored against standard peers or published in this release.

## Currency (RESOLVED 2026-10-06)
The business confirmed that **all IVT activity is AUD**. Migration V008 approved AUD for 740 carrier codes (every code in `AFSXchange.dbo.Carriers` plus the current `Sell_Side`). A carrier code that appears later without a `ML.CurrencyMap` row is `UNKNOWN` and cannot publish until a row is added.

### Original assessment (kept for the record)
No source exposes a currency. Carriers prefixed `NZ_` and `NZPOST` appear to be New Zealand. Until `ML.CurrencyMap` is approved:
* every amount is `CurrencyStatus = 'UNKNOWN'`
* peer groups are keyed by carrier and carrier group, so amounts from different carriers are never compared, but the currency label is unconfirmed
* explanations print "(currency not confirmed)"
* with `RequireKnownCurrencyForPublication = 1` (the default), nothing can publish
